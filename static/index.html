How to read this prediction +

Times include the full solar disk, Earth curvature, topocentric solar position, standard atmospheric refraction, and a sampled terrain horizon. Sunrise is the first appearance and sunset is the last disappearance of the selected local day; the Sun can disappear and reappear behind ridges.

A second on the clock is not a promise of second-level accuracy. Clouds, variable refraction, trees, buildings, DEM errors, and narrow ridges can change the observed sunset. This is a terrain and clear-sky simulation, not a photograph or live weather forecast. Polar terrain uses a NOAA mosaic; a local GeoTIFF can override online data.
